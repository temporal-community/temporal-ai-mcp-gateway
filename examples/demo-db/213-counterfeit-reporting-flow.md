## Problem

Rights holders report counterfeit listings by email to a general address. Reports are handled inconsistently, there is no record of what was removed and why, and we have twice removed a legitimate listing on an unsubstantiated report.

## Proposal

A structured intake with evidence requirements and a documented process both ways.

- A rights holder portal requiring proof of rights and specific listing identification
- A defined response time, with removal pending review for clear cases
- Seller notification with the reason and a counter-notice path
- A record of every report and outcome, retained for the statutory period

## Scope

Intellectual property reports. Safety and legality reports follow a separate and faster path.

## Risks

Removal pending review is the right default for the buyer and is routinely abused by competitors in marketplaces like ours. The counter-notice path needs to be fast and genuinely available, and repeat unfounded reporters need consequences, or the process becomes a tool for suppressing competition.

## Measurement

Reports processed within the response time, counter-notices raised and upheld, listings restored after removal, and reporters with a high unfounded rate.

## Staffing

Reading a rights claim is not the same skill as handling a support ticket. Two trained reviewers cover the current sixty reports a month with room for the increase a working portal brings, since a usable intake attracts reports that go nowhere today. A counter-notice goes to different eyes than the original removal decision, which is what makes the path genuinely available rather than nominally.

## Support Impact

Sellers hear about a removal from us rather than from a buyer who could not find the listing, and that notification is what the support queue feels first. It has to carry the reason, the reporter's identity where the law allows it, and the counter-notice link in one message, because a seller told only that a listing is gone opens a ticket to find out why.

- A template per outcome: removed, restored, report rejected
- A read-only support view of the report record, so a responder can answer without routing to review
- The unfounded-rate figure kept internal, since showing a seller a reporter's record starts a different argument

## Timeline

Portal and report record first, eight weeks, with reports still worked by hand behind it so the intake can be judged before the process is rebuilt around it. Seller notification and the counter-notice path in the following six. Removal pending review switches on only when both are live, since turning it on earlier hands competitors exactly the tool the risk above names.
