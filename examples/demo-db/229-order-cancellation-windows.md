## Context

Buyers can cancel until the seller marks an order as shipped, which sellers can do at any point, including immediately. Some sellers mark shipped on receipt of the order to close the cancellation window, and buyers experience this as being trapped.

## Proposal

A fixed cancellation window independent of seller action.

- One hour of unconditional cancellation from order placement, regardless of status
- Shipped status requiring a tracking reference, which makes early marking harder
- Cancellation after the window by seller agreement, as now, with a response commitment
- Sellers with high pre-window shipping marks flagged for review

## Scope

Physical goods. Digital items are delivered immediately and have their own refund policy.

## Risks

Sellers who genuinely pack and ship within the hour, of whom there are some, are forced to hold orders or absorb cancellations after dispatch. A short window limits the harm; the tracking requirement is the part that will generate the most seller complaint, including legitimate complaint from those using services without tracking.

## Measurement

Cancellations within the window, disputes citing inability to cancel, pre-window shipped marks, and seller complaints about the tracking requirement.

## Rollout

The one-hour window ships first and alone. It is the part buyers feel, it needs no change from sellers, and it gives a clean read on how many cancellations the window absorbs. The tracking requirement follows a quarter later, announced with the flagged-seller review already running, so the sellers it bites hardest are the ones already under review. Shipping both together would put every seller complaint and every shift in buyer behavior inside one measurement window, and we would not be able to separate them.

## Support Impact

Both sides contact us, for different reasons and on different days.

- Buyers in the first weeks, asking why an order placed ninety minutes ago cannot be cancelled
- Sellers at the tracking cutover, including those who ship by untracked post and have no path
- Disputes citing an inability to cancel should fall, and that is the number to watch rather than total contacts

## What Would Change Our Mind

If pre-window shipped marks do not fall once tracking is required, the marking behavior is not about closing the window and the flagged-seller review is pointed at the wrong thing. The other case is timing. An hour was chosen to cover the ordered-by-mistake cancellation, and if cancellations cluster against the end of the window rather than in the first ten minutes, the window is shorter than what buyers are actually doing.
